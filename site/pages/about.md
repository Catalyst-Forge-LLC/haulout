A haul-out brings a boat out of the water onto land. HaulOut brings the conversation you are viewing onto your disk. It exports one open thread and is not a bulk archive or cloud sync service.

## What it is not

Not a bulk archive. Not a cloud sync. Not a rewriter. Not affiliated with the chat sites.
