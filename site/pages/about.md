A **haul-out** is when a boat leaves the water and sits on your land. The conversation lives in their origin. HaulOut pulls it out and sets it on disk.

The first sentence on every surface says **conversation**, not keel.

## Names

| Use | String |
| --- | --- |
| Product | HaulOut |
| Button / verb | Haul out |
| Domain | haulout.dev |
| Family | Haul out the thread. Then detangle it. |

Do not drift into rescue, takeout, leftovers, or a moving-truck “haul.”

## What it is not

Not a bulk archive. Not a cloud sync. Not a rewriter. Not affiliated with the chat sites.
